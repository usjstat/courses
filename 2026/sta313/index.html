Course overview

COURSE OVERVIEW

From uncertainty to defensible decisions

The course is built around a simple question: when several actions are available and the future is not fully known, how should we choose?

Students first learn to formulate the decision problem clearly. Only then do we introduce a decision rule and interpret what that rule means in context.

What we will study

  • Decision alternatives, states of nature, consequences, payoff and loss tables
  • Decision making under certainty, uncertainty, and risk
  • Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret, Hurwicz, and Laplace criteria
  • Expected Monetary Value, Expected Opportunity Loss, and EVPI
  • Bayesian updating, posterior decisions, and value of sample information
  • Decision trees and rollback analysis for sequential decisions
  • Utility, certainty equivalents, and risk attitudes

Prerequisites

Probability and Distribution Theory I & II, and Inferential Statistics.

Learning approach. Each week starts with a decision problem. We identify the actions, uncertain events, and consequences before introducing the mathematical rule used to choose among the actions.

Intended learning outcomes

INTENDED LEARNING OUTCOMES

What you should be able to do

1

Formulate statistical decision problems by identifying alternatives, states of nature, consequences, and available information.

2

Apply and interpret decision criteria under uncertainty and risk, including expected value, opportunity loss, and value of information.

3

Use Bayes’ theorem, posterior probabilities, Bayes decision rules, and decision trees for sequential decision problems.

4

Evaluate decisions under risk using utility functions and explain how risk attitudes can alter an optimal decision.

Weekly lessons

15-WEEK LEARNING PATH

One focused lecture each week, supported by independent problem solving and reading.

Week 01

Introduction to Statistical Decision Theory

Decision problems, alternatives, states of nature, consequences, payoff/loss tables, and the decision-theory process.

Week 02

Decision-Making Environments

Certainty, uncertainty, risk, and formulation of statistical decision problems.

Week 03

Decision under Uncertainty I

Maximax and Maximin criteria for payoff and cost tables.

Week 04

Decision under Uncertainty II

Minimax Regret, Hurwicz, and Laplace criteria.

Week 05

Decision under Risk I

Expected Monetary Value and optimal action under known probabilities.

Week 06

Decision under Risk II

Expected Opportunity Loss and Expected Value of Perfect Information.

Week 07

Extensions of Expected-Value Decisions

Maximum likelihood, continuous outcomes, salvage values, and applications.

Week 08

Prior Information and Bayesian Updating

Priors, likelihoods, Bayes’ theorem, and posterior probabilities.

Week 09

Bayes Decision Rule & Sample Information

Prior and posterior decisions, value and cost of sample information.

Week 10

Bayesian Decision Analysis

Posterior expected payoff/loss and the practical value of additional information.

Week 11

Decision Trees I

Decision nodes, chance nodes, branches, outcomes, probabilities, costs, and payoffs.

Week 12

Decision Trees II

Rollback analysis, expected values, optimal choices, and sequential decisions.

Week 13

Decision Trees III

Sample information, revised probabilities, and multi-stage decisions.

Week 14

Utility Theory I

Utility of money, certainty equivalent, expected utility, and limits of EMV.

Week 15

Utility Theory II & Integrated Analysis

Risk attitudes, utility-based decisions, and synthesis of decision criteria.
